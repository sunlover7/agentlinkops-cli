# Stage 2b — the invisible-character scan

**Required on every page, every time, before it ships.** It is part of stage 2
(humanize), and it is the half of stage 2 that a human reviewer physically cannot do.

## What it is

A vendored copy of the Layer A engine from
[watermarks-remover](https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover) (MIT),
pinned in `UPSTREAM.json`, plus `wm-scan.py`, our wrapper that walks content roots
and reports line numbers.

```bash
python3 wm-scan.py <path> [<path> ...]     # report, exit 1 on findings
python3 wm-scan.py --fix <path>            # clean in place, then re-verify
python3 wm-scan.py --json <path>           # for a gate
python3 verify-vendor.py                   # prove the engine is the pinned upstream one
./revendor.sh [ref]                        # refresh from upstream and re-pin
```

## Why it is required rather than recommended

Because it catches a defect class that survives every other check we have. These
characters are invisible in the editor, invisible in review, invisible in the
rendered page, and they ride in on ordinary copy-paste from PDFs, Word and model
output. What they break is everything that reads our prose as text:

**A zero-width space inside "affidavit" means the banned-phrase scan, the citation
audits and every `grep` in the repo stop seeing the word.** The page looks fine and
the checks that are supposed to guard it have quietly stopped applying.

It is not a theoretical risk. First run across the estate, tax-sale, trucking,
coverage and proxy repos, 2026-08-18:

| repo | finding |
|---|---|
| settled | a soft hyphen inside `affi[U+00AD]davit` in rendered prose, plus 4 more in Missouri state data |
| Tax Sale Atlas | Wingdings/PUA glyphs (`U+F0B7`, `U+F073`) in 5 counties' `sale.json`, including inside an `evidence` string |
| covered-weight | 1,200+ PUA glyphs across the archived payer-policy extractions |
| haulhandbook, proxy-compare | clean |

## The archive rule, which is not negotiable

**`--fix` refuses to touch anything under `sources/`, `snapshots/`, `evidence/`,
`_sources/`, `price-history/` or `raw/`.**

That tree is the evidence every `_source` and `verified_quote` resolves against. The
marks in it are glyph artifacts of a document *somebody else published*, and they are
part of what we recorded. Rewriting the archive so a scan goes green is precisely the
tampering the provenance chain exists to detect — one edited file and one re-forged
hash is the whole covered-weight story.

So: **the archive is reported and left alone. The QUOTE lifted out of it is the thing
to check.** A hit inside a quote field is a provenance question before it is a
formatting one: open the source, see what the character is, and decide. A PDF bullet
glyph rendered into a quote is noise the reader never saw and can be dropped; a real
character cannot.

## The load-bearing rule (found by auditing, 2026-08-18)

**`--fix` also holds back any file where the mark sits on a line carrying regex-replacement
syntax**, tagging it `[LOAD-BEARING? regex literal on that line - review by hand]`.

The estate-wide audit found three of these, and every one is a **sanitizer written with the
literal characters it strips**:

```js
.replace(/[<zwsp><zwnj><zwj><bom>]+/g, '')   covered-weight  extract-ehb-chart-rows.mjs:127
.replace(/&nbsp;| |<zwsp>/g, " ")            proxy-compare   scraper/vendors/proxyscrape.ts:112
.replace(/<nbsp>/g, " ")                     proxy-compare   scraper/vendors/rayobyte.ts:67
```

Auto-cleaning those empties the character class and silently breaks the guard, letting through
exactly the contamination it existed to catch. **A blanket `--fix` across the estate would have
broken three sanitizers in two repos** — the scanner would have disabled its own counterparts.

This is the argument for classify-before-fix in one example. A hit is not a defect; a hit is a
question. The three answers that mean "do not touch" are: it is archived evidence, it is a
sourced quote, or it is load-bearing.

## What we deliberately do NOT run

The upstream project does more than this, and the rest of it is off by decision, not
by oversight.

| layer | status | why |
|---|---|---|
| **A** — invisible Unicode, exotic spaces | **required** | pure contamination, deterministic, zero judgement needed |
| **Files** — EXIF / XMP on our own generated covers | available | harmless hygiene; our covers are generated by our own script, so there is little to strip |
| **C2PA manifests** | **detect, never strip** | C2PA is a provenance record. On one of our own covers it is a signal that something upstream generated an image with AI — on sites that say "never an AI image", that is a question to answer, not noise to delete |
| **B** — rewriting text to defeat statistical watermarks | **opt-in, off** | our humanize stage already rewrites AI-pattern prose on quality grounds. Making "defeat provenance watermarking" an explicit standing step across five YMYL properties is a decision for the owner, not a default |
| **Pixels** — CtrlRegen image watermark removal | **not installed** | needs a model backend, and it has no use on sites that generate their own covers. Its actual use is stripping watermarks off images we did not make |

If any of those need turning on, that is a conversation, and the reason belongs in
this file next to the change.

## Keeping it honest

`vendor/` is byte-identical to upstream and must stay that way — `verify-vendor.py`
proves it, and `revendor.sh` is how it changes. Do not edit it in place. The last time
a copied source of truth got edited locally in this estate it became a house rule
nobody agreed to, sitting in a file that looked authoritative.
