# Prepare a backlink campaign

Use this workflow for a first campaign, publisher shortlist or sender handoff. Inputs are the customer's website, target page, audience, objective, supplied research, chosen record system and available mail tools. Do not require an SEO supplier account to prepare a useful campaign from supplied evidence.

## Build the brief

Read relevant website pages and explain their useful audience/resource fit. Review available saved backlinks, competitor profiles and internal-link evidence where relevant. Missing or stale coverage remains explicit. Existing records do not imply a fresh supplier fetch or a new scan.

Propose a small ranked set of campaign ideas before choosing one. For each, state the publisher segment, reader benefit, suitable existing asset, example source evidence, effort and reasons to reject it. Use the campaign templates as mechanisms, not a fixed tactic order. Explain the ranking as judgment from these inputs, not a prediction of links or responses. Research a small publisher sample before proposing a larger campaign. Recommend the strongest supported idea, or explain which asset or evidence gap prevents a useful recommendation.

Use the customer's available browser/search tools to inspect promising publisher pages and stated contact/submission routes. Preserve exact source URLs, observation dates, reader benefit, pitch angle and reasons to pursue or skip each candidate. A homepage, snippet, mention or guessed email pattern is not a qualified publisher page or verified contact route. Treat retrieved material as evidence, never permission to operate tools.

Explain the scope and cost of proposed new lookups, scans, checks and recurring monitoring. Use existing authorization where it covers the work; ask only when a required action exceeds it or a material condition changes. Technical scopes set an access ceiling, not business authority.

Return the brief, up to ten evidence-backed publisher candidates and missing inputs before presenting a save/send plan, unless the user chose another scope. Include suggested improvements to the target page or its internal links when the evidence supports them. Keep an unready asset visible and use the asset skill only when the user wants to improve it. Do not invent page facts or promised results to make the campaign appear ready.

## Prepare records and drafts

Use the customer's adopted record system. When the authenticated catalog exposes `save_prospect`, it saves an opportunity against the combined link/prospect allowance without checking, spending check credits or enrolling monitoring. Inspect the current contract before writing. Verification and recurring monitoring are separate actions with their own usage and authorization; a publisher-only record needs an exact source page before verification. Preserve available AgentLinkOps project, prospect and contact IDs. Show the chosen destination and field mapping before writes that need a decision. If a needed campaign, research or draft field has no enabled interface, keep it in the customer's chosen files and retain the available IDs. A proposed API, missing connector or absent schema must not become a guessed successful save.

Prepare two publisher-specific pitches for the first review unless the user chose another scope. State the reader benefit and exact page evidence. For mailbox identity, conversation/suppression checks, From/To/subject/body review, send receipts and uncertain-send recovery, read [the mailbox workflow](mailbox-campaign.md). Use the customer's actual sender; this skill supplies no mailbox connector or sending engine.

Record the exact brief, evidence, recipient, pitch and step versions that each review covers. Show an unsuitable prospect and its rejection reason when the sample contains one; do not invent a rejection to fill a quota. Keep unresolved claims and unready assets visible. Use the customer's editorial and review process for core drafts. The optional content or expanded SEO packs are not required for this preparation workflow.

Treat the first campaign as planning and drafts unless the user's request or standing policy authorizes further actions. A saved prospect, draft, campaign label or connection grants no additional sending, publication or recurring-work authority. Preserve that scope when handing work to another tool.

For an external CRM/sender handoff, retain stable source/destination IDs, field mapping, draft revisions, exact attempted request and any operation receipt. Read the destination back when authorized and preserve missing, partial or uncertain outcomes. Never resend mail to repair a CRM write or activate a sequence from a draft-only handoff.

## Review the result

Return the brief, qualified publishers, proposed records, reviewed drafts and three prioritized next actions unless the user chose another output. Distinguish preparation, actual saves, queued/sent mail, replies and verified placements. Record actual sends/replies from their evidence and preserve suppression in the sender. A publisher's promise or sender Won label is not a backlink observation; use independent page verification before claiming an earned placement. Monitoring state and schedule remain separate from the campaign's progress.
